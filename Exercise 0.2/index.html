This simple website introduces appliance energy use in the Australian market. It focuses on how everyday choices can affect household electricity bills.

Energy Use at Home

Common appliances such as televisions, fridges, washing machines and heaters all contribute to household energy consumption. In Australia, energy rating labels help shoppers compare products before buying.

Choosing efficient appliances can reduce running costs over time. It can also lower the environmental impact of daily electricity use.

FAQ

What is appliance energy consumption?

It is the amount of electricity an appliance uses while operating. This is often measured in kilowatt-hours.

Why do energy ratings matter?

Energy ratings make it easier to compare similar appliances. A more efficient model may cost less to run.

How can households reduce energy use?

Households can switch appliances off when not in use, compare energy labels, and choose suitable appliance sizes.
