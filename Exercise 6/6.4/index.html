TV Energy Consumption

Energy Consumption for Different TV Screen Types and Sizes

Most TVs on sale in Australia are LED and they vary considerably in energy consumption. Use the filter buttons to compare screen technologies in the histogram, then hover over points in the scatterplot to see each TV's screen size.

Histogram of Labelled Energy Consumption

Energy Consumption by Star Rating

The scatterplot shows each TV model as a point. Star rating is shown on the x-axis, labelled energy consumption is shown on the y-axis, and colour shows the screen technology. Hover over a point to display the TV screen size.
